What we make

Original stories

Every piece starts from a real event in our own build. No templates with the words swapped and no borrowed voices.

Rights first

Each story carries a rights record listing whose voice, likeness and material it uses, with consent attached, plus clear AI disclosure.

Checked, not assumed

Someone other than the maker checks every output before it moves on, and the whole process can restart from where it stopped.

The series

  1. We find a story worth telling and clear its rights.
  2. We produce it with the AI tools we actually use, and show how.
  3. An independent check approves it before it's published.
  4. We cut the long piece into short clips and track what each one earns.

The series teaches that loop, so creators and businesses can run their own studio. See the tools we use.
